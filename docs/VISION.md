## The game

**Bare Hands 3D** — a stylized noir, open-world crime game set in **Cinder
City**, built for the phone first. You start with nothing but your fists.
Everything that matters costs money, and money is life. The city is wet,
neon-lit, and remembers what you do in it.

## Pillars

1. **Fists first.** The hero's identity is bare-knuckle combat — heavy,
   stylish, cinematic. Weapons come later and always carry weight and cost.
2. **Money is life.** The signature system is **The Bullet Ledger**: every
   bullet costs real cash. Violence earns money with style; spending money
   on violence is a decision, never reflex.
3. **The city remembers you.** Reputation per district, heat from public
   violence, witnesses, graffiti with your nickname. A living noir city,
   not a backdrop.
4. **Premium on a phone.** Cinematic camera, wet neon night, punches that
   hit — 60 seconds to feel "real game". 30+ FPS on iPhone 13 mini (Low
   tier), best on iPhone 16 (High tier).

## Hard rules

- Godot 4.7, Mobile renderer, touch-first, landscape.
- Plain-text project: `.gd`, `.tscn`, `project.godot`. Small files. Everything pushed.
- No other games' characters, art, names, music or code.
- Assets only CC0 or free-for-commercial-use, each listed with source and
  license in `CREDITS.md`.
- Never claim the game *looks* or *feels* good: the agent cannot see a
  phone screen. Phone-only claims wait for the owner's test.

## Series arc

- **Series 1 (done)** — look and feel: stability, hero + cinematic camera,
  wet neon night, punches that hit.
- **Series 2 (this)** — sound, soul and depth: audio foundation, adaptive
  music, combat depth, the Bullet Ledger, economy and heat, a city that
  remembers, a cast with faces, the cinematic opening.
- **Later (explicitly out of scope now)** — driving, full gunplay, multiplayer.
